What the expression-only maps got wrong

On Visium HD, only 25% of a spot’s 8 spatial neighbors shared its scLDL type; 29% of bins were fully isolated. DG and astrocytes were coherent; cortical layers, microglia, and subiculum were salt-and-pepper. Entropy was already doing the right thing (0.88 at boundaries vs 0.43 in interiors). On Slide-seqV2, RCTD singlet accuracy was 97% in spatial interiors and 18% on isolated beads — almost all of the disagreement with RCTD lived in speckles, not in CA1/CA3/DG bands.

How spatial scLDL is wired

AnnotationPipeline(model="scldl", spatial="auto"). If the query has obsm["spatial"] or x/y columns, isolated spots (≤1 of 8 neighbors sharing the hard type) are snapped toward the majority of a bilateral spatial graph (near in space and similar in the reference PCA). Interiors are left as expression scLDL so cortical layers cannot be eaten by a growing majority. scRNA holdouts skip this path. spatial="off" restores the old maps.
